The theology of this app

Commitment is not contrary to God's will. It is one of the main ways you walk in it. God steers moving ships — He redirects people who are going somewhere, not people frozen at the harbor.

Being redirected is not the same as being wrong. A commitment released back to God in obedience is a completed act of faithfulness, not a failed decision. That's why this app has a “Released” state with honor, not shame.

Clarity is a kindness to others. When you can say “Because I believe…, I have decided…, and I hold it with open hands,” people hear conviction with humility — not confusion. You don't owe everyone an explanation, but you can always have one.

Speed is spiritual. Most decisions don't need more information; they need courage. If the four gates are open, prolonged hesitation is usually fear of people, not reverence for God. Decide, then facilitate: work hard, do it well, keep reviewing in prayer.
